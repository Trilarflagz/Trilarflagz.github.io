Jingqi Tian (田景绮)

Hi! I am a second-year master at Tsinghua University, majoring in Data Science and Information Technology (DSIT). I expect to graduate in 2028. I am fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Yansong Tang in IVG@SZ group.

Previously, I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Sichuan University in 2025.

My research interests lie in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), Reinforcement Learning, and Video Reasoning.
